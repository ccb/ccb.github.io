# Speaker Introduction

*For hosts introducing Chris Callison-Burch at invited talks. About 75 seconds when read aloud.*

Chris Callison-Burch is the Raj and Neera Singh Professor of Artificial Intelligence at the University of Pennsylvania. His research is in natural language processing, and his group currently works on using large language models to verify scientific claims, to analyze bias in news coverage, and to generate synthetic training data. He leads Penn's effort on the DARPA SciFy program. He has published more than 250 papers, cited over 40,000 times, and is a Sloan Research Fellow. He has served as General Chair of ACL and Program Co-Chair of EMNLP, and in 2023 he testified before Congress on generative AI and copyright. He also received Penn's highest teaching honor, the Lindback Award, this year. Please welcome Chris.
